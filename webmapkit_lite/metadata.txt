about=Web Map Kit Lite turns your QGIS layers into a fast, mobile-friendly web map and publishes it in one click to your own free Cloudflare R2 storage: no server, no subscription, no coding. It exports vector tiles (one PMTiles file) with a MapLibre viewer that keeps your QGIS colours, categories, graduated and proportional symbols, labels and field aliases, with popups, a legend with layer toggles, and search. Publish as many maps as you like; publishing again with the same web address updates the map at the same link. Maps show a small "Made with Web Map Kit" link. The full edition, Web Map Kit (paid, https://mapship.link/webmap-kit/), adds photos in popups, your own domain, a list of all published maps, and no badge. Cloudflare R2's free tier includes 10 GB of storage with no bandwidth fees; Cloudflare asks for a payment card when R2 is enabled.
